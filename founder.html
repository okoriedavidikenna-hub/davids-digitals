FOUNDER • CEO • TECHNOLOGY BUILDER

Building technology with purpose.

OKORIE DAVID IKENNA is the Founder and Chief Executive Officer of DAVIDS DIGITALS LTD.© , a technology-focused digital company built around technology, creativity, and innovation.

With a strong interest in emerging technologies and digital creation, David founded DAVIDS DIGITALS in September 2026 with the ambition of building useful digital products and solutions for the future.

His areas of interest include technology, artificial intelligence, web development, and graphic design. Through these areas, he aims to combine creativity and technology to transform ideas into practical digital experiences.
